## Downselect Result: [VTBD, all concepts & output indicator](https://github.com/marian-scientific/proposals/tree/Christ/RFP002%20-%20MCA/submitted%20proposals/VTBD) & [Alarm-Machine, Approach 2](https://github.com/marian-scientific/proposals/tree/Christ/RFP002%20-%20MCA/submitted%20proposals/Alarm-Machine)

#### Conditions of bid approval:
The primitive output indicator described in the VTBD proposal will be used as the benchmark for all technical timing solutions. The VTBD applicant will provide the Alarm-Machine applicant with details regarding actuation conditions by March 28, 2025. Approach 2 from the Alarm-Machine as well as all the primitive concepts from the VTBD proposal represent novel solutions that the organization sees long-term value in pursuing. All other approaches will not be funded nor expected in the final documentation.

#### Reasoning: 

Both bids indicate a combined proposed budget at or below the approved internal funding levels and a deliverable timeline at or ahead of the approved internal final deliverable date.

The technical downselect was non-competitive due to lack of bids and low combined proposed budgets. The proposals cover a wide variety of novel solutions that the organization deems worth investigating.

All applicants will be notified of this result on March 17, 2025.
