## Downselect Result: [LEVI](https://github.com/marian-scientific/proposals/tree/Christ/RFP001%20-%20HOHMO/submitted%20proposals/LEVI) & [IRON-JAR](https://github.com/marian-scientific/proposals/tree/Christ/RFP001%20-%20HOHMO/submitted%20proposals/IRON-JAR)

#### Reasoning: 

Both bids indicate a combined proposed budget at or below the approved internal funding levels and a deliverable timeline at or ahead of the approved internal final deliverable date.

The technical downselect was non-competitive due to lack of bids and low combined proposed budgets.

All applicants will be notified of this result on February 22, 2025.
