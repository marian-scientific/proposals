# RFP001 - Human-Observable Homemade Microcontroller Output

posted February 19, 2025

This Request for Proposals refers to the HOHMO prototype project, which covers the design, fabrication & testing of a human-observable homemade electrical device to be used as a general purpose output indicator for a microcontroller. This RFP is deliberately terse in order to foster a diversity of creative solutions. The following definitions apply:

1. human-observable: having a characteristic state change that can be observed by a human sense (sight, sound, smell, touch or taste)
2. homemade: being producible from "raw materials" with equipment generally available to the average person
3. raw material: (subjective) substance of limited prior processing (examples: wire, metal, wood, resin, glue, resistor; NOT diode, transistor, relay)
4. microcontroller output: (GPIO) an electrical device which can be driven by ~0.015A at a 3.3V logic level

Proposals for this project will be accepted until midnight (UTC-5) on February 21st, 2025. To be considered, all proposals require:

  * a preliminary schedule indicating dates for all intermediate and final deliverables
  * an estimated program budget with line items for individual raw materials and design/fabrication labor
  * a coherent solution with an actionable design and fabrication plan

The ideal proposal will succinctly describe a novel solution with one or more illustrative graphics.

All budgets, schedules, and solutions are subject to final approval, even after a proposal and contractor are selected. Results of the proposal downselect will be distributed to all applicants on February 22nd, 2025.
